Holos Bedside + Grid

The sixth Holos prototype, playable in the browser. When someone asks, the board offers only the answers that fit. When the answer isn't there, or nobody is asking, the whole board is one press away.

What changed from Bedside. “Something else” now opens Holos Grid's whole board: every topic, the urgent quick phrases and a message to build. After the story the whole board is home, so the person can start a conversation without being asked.

A browser sketch, not the app. The Swift prototype is Prototypes/HolosBedsideGrid.swiftpm in this repository. Here there is no vibration and no head control, and speech uses this browser's voices: Ukrainian is spoken only if your device has a Ukrainian voice.
